Magic Presents: Reality Echoes · Oct 16 – Nov 5

Echoed pairs

Every Play Booster has three bonus-sheet legends: one true echoed pair and one unpaired legend.

  • Before each game, you may reveal two different cards from one echoed pair. They start the game in exile.
  • You may cast one of them as though it were in your hand. Then you may shuffle the other into your library.
  • One pair per game. Two copies of the same card don't count. Don't force a third color for one.

Draft winner gets a Reality Fracture set symbol pin, while supplies last.

What's new on the cards

Empower Jace NPut N loyalty on your Jace token. No token yet? Make one first. You only ever build up one token.
PrepareThe creature carries a small spell. While it's prepared, pay that spell's cost to cast it once. The creature stays.
HeartwoodArtifact token. Taps for red or green, every turn. Not a Treasure, you don't sacrifice it.
Surveil & scryLook at the top cards. Surveil can bin them, scry can bottom them. Neither one draws.

The set's main character

Your Jace token

  • −1: surveil 1. −3: draw a card.
  • One ability per turn, in your main phase. Adding loyalty doesn't give you a second use.
  • It can't block, and your opponent can attack it. Protect the board first.

When the pack hits you

The ten-second pick check

  1. Can I play early? Check your two- and three-drops before taking another expensive reward.
  2. What enables this? Name the card that makes the payoff work. A second payoff isn't an enabler.
  3. What answers their threat? Keep removal. Check whether it hits planeswalkers too.
  4. Can I use both picks? Two cards that look like a pair can want different decks.

Wizards' deckbuilding order

Build from threats, then removal

  1. Find your threats, the cards that win on their own.
  2. Add removal. Counterspells and discard count.
  3. Play your two best colors. Three stretches your mana.

At the table

  • Keep hands with a route into the game. Lands plus a play by turn 2 or 3 beats lands plus bombs.
  • Attacking Jace or the player? Pick the one that stops them winning. Don't send everything at Jace if you have lethal.
  • No notes or apps while drafting, building, or playing. Not sure about a rule? Ask.
